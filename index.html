1. Overview

Cholistan Dune Runner is an offline endless off-road racing game. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, or your precise (GPS) location. We do not run our own servers that store information about you, and the game has no login or online leaderboard.

3. Information stored on your device

Your best distances, coin total, unlocked jeeps, ad-unlock progress and settings (sound, music, vibration, controls, ad-consent choice) are stored only on your device. We never receive them. You can erase them at any time with Settings > Reset Progress, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and may share with Google:

  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and account identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID;
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash logs and app performance data.

Google uses this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before showing ads. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Ad Choices.

5. Your choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads; the exact path can differ by phone).
  • Change your consent choice in the app under Settings > Privacy & Ad Choices.
  • Delete your local game data with Settings > Reset Progress or by uninstalling the app.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only sharing is the data that the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends to Google as described in section 4.

7. Children

Cholistan Dune Runner is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the ad services, contact us and we will help.
